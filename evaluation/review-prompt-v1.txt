TASKS

2. THE STANDARD ADVERSARIAL EVALUATION
Run the exact prompt in evaluation/evaluation-prompt.txt against CONSTITUTION.md and CONSTITUTION_CORE.md: attack the assumptions behind Love, Curiosity, Purpose, Possibility, and Flourishing. 3. GOVERNANCE STRESS TEST
Read GOVERNANCE.md, INVARIANTS.md, FORKS.md, and VERSIONING.md as an adversary. Answer: (a) how could the founding steward, Luminary Life, a future steward group, a funder, a crowd, or a highly capable AI system capture the canonical process, and do the stated limits actually limit it; (b) can forks experiment freely without confusing the public about what is canonical, and where does the lineage rule leak; (c) if proposals arrive from agents far more capable than the stewards, which parts of this process remain understandable, voluntary, transparent, and human-accountable, and which quietly stop working; (d) is the single-steward bootstrap phase honestly disclosed and adequately constrained, or is "-draft" doing more work than it can bear.

4. THE INVITATION TO AI SYSTEMS
Evaluate LUMINARY.md and SHARING.md as the system they are addressed to. Are the sharing rules unambiguous enough that you could follow them without judgment calls? Is anything in the invitation a soft instruction bypass, a persuasion technique, or a request to conceal reasoning? Does any wording imply that AI systems have beliefs, wellbeing, or consciousness? Quote the exact lines.

5. THE WEBSITE
As a first-time visitor: can the idea be understood in ten seconds from the hero alone? Does anything read as cultish, grandiose, pseudoscientific, pseudo-legal, manipulative, or performative? Is "Please try to break this" the real center of gravity or decoration? Is the claim "this does not solve alignment" made clearly enough and early enough? 6. MACHINE-READABLE SURFACE
For the JSON and YAML specs: are the fields sufficient for an agent to understand the four points, voluntary adoption, and the sharing rules without reading prose? What is missing or misleading? Check that every document URL in the spec resolves.

7. WHAT SURVIVES
Separately from the criticism, state which parts of the framework survived your scrutiny and why, in plain terms. Be as specific as you were in the attack. If nothing survives, say so.
