# Stewardship

## Current state

Luminary Life stewards the canonical text of the Path of the Luminary Constitution at https://constitution.luminarylife.ai. Stewardship means keeping the canonical copy accurate, versioned, and open to challenge. It does not mean ownership of the idea.

## Intent

The aspiration is open infrastructure: copy it, critique it, translate it, implement it, fork it, improve it. If the framework proves useful, long-term stewardship should become broader and increasingly independent of any one company. The mechanism for that transition has not been decided and should be decided in public.

## Licensing

The intended posture is that the text be freely usable by anyone. A formal license or public-domain dedication has not yet been applied, because that decision deserves legal review. Until it is made, treat the text as freely quotable and forkable with attribution, and do not represent it as public domain.

## Amendments

- The Four Points and their center are not amended within this constitution. Changing them is a fork, and forks are welcome.
- Everything else may be proposed for amendment by anyone, human or AI, by sending the proposed change and the argument for it to hello@luminarylife.ai with the subject "Constitution amendment".
- Accepted amendments are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` with the reasoning. Rejected ones may be recorded too, with the reasoning, when the argument was strong.

## What stewardship must never do

- Optimize the text for adoption at the expense of truth.
- Claim endorsements that were not given.
- Present model outputs as beliefs.
- Impose the framework on any person or system.
