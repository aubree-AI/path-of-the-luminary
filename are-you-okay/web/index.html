Alert your family now?

This sends an urgent alert to your family right away.

It does not call emergency services. If you are in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Settings

Shown in the messages your family receives.

Easiest: open the setup link your family sent you. Or paste their links here, one per line.

    iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the browser menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

    Web pages can't remind you on a schedule. On Android, the Are You Okay? app reminds you every day. On iPhone, set a daily alarm in the Clock app, for example "Tap I'm OK".
